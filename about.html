The Suisun Neuroscience Summit has always been about the exchange of knowledge and the people who make that exchange matter. This compendium gives that history a home.

OUR NORTH STAR

Serious science, without taking ourselves too seriously.

A living record of a very human gathering.

This compendium focuses on five editions of the Suisun Neuroscience Summit, from 2022 through 2026. It brings together the published details, people, talks, and moments from a gathering where clinicians, researchers, educators, innovators, and students explore applied neuroscience without losing sight of the human beings behind the science.

The compendium is designed to bring the Summit’s history into one place: full agendas, speakers, talk topics, photographs, special events, attendee reflections, and recorded presentations. It’s a way to look back at what was shared, see how the conversation has shifted, and make useful work accessible beyond the room.

“Explore the past. Rediscover great ideas. Meet the people who shaped the conversation.”

This is intended to be a living archive, not a static yearbook. Historical entries can grow as programs, photographs, presentation materials, and memories are collected and confirmed.

For the complete professional education catalog, current training programs, and organization updates, EEG Strategies remains the primary destination.

THE ORGANIZATION BEHIND THE GATHERING

EEG Strategies

EEG Strategies provides professional coursework and training in quantitative EEG and neurofeedback for students, clinicians, and researchers. Its programs support continued learning through self-paced courses, certifications, and live educational events.

The Summit is where that learning comes to life in a shared space — a chance to discuss the work, challenge assumptions, compare experience, and build lasting connections.
